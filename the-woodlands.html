Montgomery County offers a deep Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Texas and the full Part D lineup. Houston Methodist The Woodlands, Memorial Hermann The Woodlands, St. Luke’s The Woodlands and MD Anderson The Woodlands each contract separately, and HCA’s Conroe hospital adds a fifth.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. A plan leaving your county opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in The Woodlands?

Montgomery County typically offers a deep Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Texas, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

Is MD Anderson The Woodlands in my Advantage plan?

MD Anderson contracts with some Advantage plans and not others, changing year to year, and accepts Original Medicare and any Medigap policy. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.
