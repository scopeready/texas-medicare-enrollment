Medicare in Corpus Christi

What to know before you compare plans in Nueces County

Nueces County offers a moderate Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Texas and the full Part D lineup. Christus Spohn and HCA’s Corpus Christi Medical Center contract with different carriers, and NAS Corpus Christi and NAS Kingsville retirees carry TRICARE For Life, which changes the calculation.

Rockport, Port Aransas and Aransas Pass fill with Winter Texans each fall whose plans from home may cover only emergencies here; our Winter Texans guide covers which plans travel.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. A plan leaving your county opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Corpus Christi?

Nueces County typically offers a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Texas, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

Is Christus Spohn in my plan’s network?

Christus Spohn and Corpus Christi Medical Center contract with different Advantage plans, changing year to year. With Original Medicare and a Medigap policy there is no network. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.
