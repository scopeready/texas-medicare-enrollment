What to know before you compare plans in Williamson County

Williamson County offers a wide Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Texas and the full Part D lineup. St. David’s Georgetown, Baylor Scott & White Round Rock and Ascension Seton Williamson each contract with different carriers, and Austin’s specialists are twenty minutes down I-35.

Sun City residents travel more than most and many keep a second home; our Winter Texans guide covers which plans travel. We compare what is sold at your ZIP code with your doctors and calendar in hand.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. A plan leaving your county opens a Special Enrollment Period.
